# Sony CXD5610 GNSS receiver

Sony's public GNSS page lists the CXD5610GF as a dual-band L1/L5 receiver with
1.8-V I/O, support for GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou, QZSS, and NavIC, and
low-power operation. The page exposes the public product summary and links to
a data sheet behind a registration form; the form was not bypassed.

- [Sony GNSS product page](https://www.sony-semicon.com/en/products/lsi-ic/gps.html)
- [Sony data-sheet download form](https://info.sony-semicon.com/en/gnss/datasheet-form0001)

## Sapporo evidence

The Sapporo firmware names `CXD5610GF-driver.cpp`, opens logical UART1 at
921600 baud, and emits the observed `$PSS`/`@VER` startup exchange. The
emulator therefore models the observed startup transport, not the full GNSS
receiver firmware or navigation solution.

The exact CXD5610GF versus CXD5610GG package on the watch is unresolved. No
private Sony data sheet or firmware bytes are copied here.
